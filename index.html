THESIS · IN PROGRESS01 / 04

VERDE

Smart hydroponics monitoring and control

VERDE brings sensor monitoring, plant image analysis, and rule-based decisions into one Raspberry Pi–based system. The thesis scope covers pH, EC, temperature, humidity, and turbidity, with dashboard monitoring, data logs, alerts, and automated dosing.

The architecture below follows our thesis diagrams. The four plant states are Healthy, Yellowing, Wilting, and Harvest-Ready; the public three-class experiment is a separate earlier study.

Raspberry Pi 5PythonYOLOv8IoTSensors

FROM THE THESIS / SYSTEM OVERVIEW

Open full diagram ↗
How the hardware connects +

Sense. EC and turbidity signals pass through voltage dividers. These signals and the pH sensor feed the ADS1115 ADC. Temperature, humidity, and camera modules provide the other inputs.

Control. The Raspberry Pi sends control signals to two relay modules for circulation and dosing. A third relay switches the EC sensor supply.

Monitor. The GSM module sends alerts. The dashboard and detection log exchange information with the Pi for monitoring, user input, and stored records.
