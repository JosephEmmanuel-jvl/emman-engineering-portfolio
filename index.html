THESIS · DEFENSE PASSED01 / 04

VERDE

Smart hydroponics monitoring and control

VERDE is our smart hydroponics thesis prototype. It combines sensor monitoring, plant image analysis, and rule-based decisions on a Raspberry Pi 5. Our thesis passed the final panel defense, and development continues.

The final thesis system covers four plant states, including Harvest-Ready. The linked public YOLOv8 repository documents an earlier three-class experiment; its result is separate from the full system.

INPUTSpH, EC, temperature, humidity, turbidity, and cameraVISIONPlant condition and harvest readinessOUTPUTSDashboard, data logs, alerts, and dosing

FROM THE THESIS / SYSTEM OVERVIEW

Open full diagram ↗
How the hardware connects +

Sense. EC and turbidity signals pass through voltage dividers. These signals and the pH sensor feed the ADS1115 ADC. Temperature, humidity, and camera modules provide the other inputs.

Control. The Raspberry Pi sends control signals to two relay modules for circulation and dosing. A third relay switches the EC sensor supply.

Monitor. The GSM module sends alerts. The dashboard and detection log exchange information with the Pi for monitoring, user input, and stored records.
